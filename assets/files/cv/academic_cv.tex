\newcommand{\ProfileText}{
I am a statistician with a BSc and MSc in Statistics from the University of Brasília (UnB). My research centres on statistical inference, uncertainty quantification, and simulation, developed through Bayesian and frequentist methods, hierarchical and spatial modelling, and computational tools including R, Python, and Stan. During my MSc, I applied these methods to problems in extreme value theory and dependence structures, working with physical and environmental data, including NASA solar irradiance measurements, alongside separate applied work with large administrative and public-sector datasets. Across these projects, my consistent interest has been how statistical modelling, computation, and uncertainty quantification can be combined to draw reliable conclusions from complex, real-world data.
}

\cventry{Feb 2024}{Jul 2026}{MSc in Statistics}{Graduate Program in Statistics, University of Brasília (UnB)}{Brasília, Brazil}{%
    \cvdetail{Thesis}{%
        \emph{``\textbf{Statistical Modelling of Spatial Extremes in Complex Systems}''}
        \\
        Developed a Bayesian hierarchical geostatistical model for spatial
        extremes with BGEV marginals, allowing all distributional
        parameters to vary spatially through latent Gaussian structures.
        The model is designed to capture bimodality, tail behaviour, and
        spatial heterogeneity within a unified probabilistic framework.
      }

    \cvdetail{Methods}{%
        Bayesian hierarchical modelling;
        Extreme Value Theory;
        latent Gaussian processes with spatial covariance;
        support-preserving parameter transformations;
        prior specification and regularisation;
        non-centred parameterisation;
        HMC/NUTS inference in Stan;
        spatial confounding control via QR projection;
        MCMC simulation and diagnostics;
        parameter recovery;
        uncertainty quantification;
        and estimation of extreme quantiles.
      }

    \cvdetail{Research}{%
        Alongside the dissertation, developed independent research in
        statistical methodology, extreme value theory, climatology, and
        astrostatistics, resulting in 4 submitted manuscripts.
        2 further manuscripts are being developed from the thesis itself,
        one methodological and one applied.
        Additional research conducted during the MSc led to 3
        oral/invited presentations and 6 scientific posters at national
        and international events.
      }

    \cvdetail{Courses}{%
        Statistical Inference,
        Bayesian Inference,
        Mathematical Statistics,
        Computational Techniques in Statistics,
        Machine Learning,
        Geostatistics,
        Extreme Value Theory and Copulas,
        Generalized Linear Models.
      }

    \cvdetail{Status}{%

      }
