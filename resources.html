Curation rule

Publish less, but publish well.

The site should feel like a laboratory notebook that has already been edited for visitors.

Do publish: final slides, final posters, abstracts, code with documentation, public preprints, stable datasets you are allowed to share, and explanatory notes that have been checked.

Do not publish: application-specific cover letters, transcripts containing personal identifiers, internal institutional data, draft manuscripts without co-author approval, private datasets, or folders full of unexplained files.
